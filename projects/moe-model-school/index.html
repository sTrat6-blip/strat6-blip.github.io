Model School Electrical Design

Two complete reference designs, a 480-pupil primary school and an 800-pupil secondary school, built to MOE's Non-Academic Education Norms, NEC 2017 and NFPA 101/72. Each comes with 3D blocks, electrical layouts, a lighting design, load calculation, panel schedules and a priced bill of quantities. A tender edition of each BOQ has the rates left blank. Contractors price what the BOQ says, so the BOQ has to be right.

SHEET 01 · WHY

Code violations recur across MOE schools

This isn't one bad contract. GEI keeps finding the same defects on site at nursery, primary and secondary schools across the regions, and the same gaps are written into MOE's bills of quantities. A contractor who prices the BOQ as written installs the defect, then GEI refuses it and the job pays twice. The model design closes each pattern at the BOQ stage.

How MOE can use this

Files issued with this design

  • Model Primary School - Bill of Quantities (Rev A) - Tender.xlsx: quantities with blank rates, ready for contractors to price
  • … Engineer's Estimate.xlsx: the priced version, for evaluating bids and checking for unbalanced rates
  • The same pair for the secondary school
  • Model Schools - Design Calculations (Rev A).xlsx: service load calculation, lighting schedule and every panel schedule
  • The calculation engine, so a new site can be re-run with its own room list

Folder: GEI\Model Designs - Homes & Schools\BOQs

Before a model goes to tender

  • Confirm GPL's secondary voltage and transformer arrangement at the service application. The transformer is carried as a Provisional Sum.
  • Re-measure feeder trench routes against the civil site plan.
  • Fix the AC scope: units are priced complete. Delete them if MOE procures AC separately.
  • Keep the units as written: metres for cable and conduit, No. for devices with the box included.
  • Send the tender edition to GEI for a design check before issue.
