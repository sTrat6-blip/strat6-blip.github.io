Government Electrical Inspectorate · Ministry of Public Works

GEI Model Homes

Twenty reference electrical designs: one- to six-bedroom homes in three specification tiers, plus two 40 A core homes. Each one is worked to NEC 2017, with its load calculation, panel schedule, plan, single-line diagram and priced bill of quantities. Use them to set the minimum a home of a given size must have, and to check what designers, contractors and BOQs submit to the Inspectorate.

SHEET 01 · STANDARD

The minimum standard by home size

Click any cell to open that model below

Each cell gives the GEI standard service size, the panel size, and the installed electrical cost including 10 % contingency. NEC sets a 100 A floor for every one-family dwelling (230.79(C)). The GEI standard adds 25 % headroom over the calculated load and requires 20 % of panel spaces to be spare. The Core column is a proposed GEI variation: 1–2 bedroom starter homes on a 40 A service, below the NEC floor, with the same 25 % headroom.
